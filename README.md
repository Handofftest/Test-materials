# The Handoff Test: test materials

These are the free test files behind two videos from The Handoff Test, a YouTube channel that gives real office work to AI tools and checks what comes back.

| Folder | Video | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| [`invoices/`](invoices/) | *I Hid 65 Mistakes in 200 Invoices. Here's What AI Caught.* | 200 synthetic invoices, the answer key, the exact instructions, the marking script, the assistants' raw answers and the method |
| [`inbox/`](inbox/) | *How to Use ChatGPT to Sort Your Inbox (Prompt Included)* | A 26-email fictional inbox, the prompts, the priority rule, a step-by-step guide, the answer key and ChatGPT's raw answers |

Everything here is **fictional test data**. No real company, person, invoice, email address or mailbox was used. Tax IDs start with `TEST-`, bank details use `TEST00` and email addresses end in `.example`.

## Reuse it
- Run the same test on another AI tool, a newer model or your own prompt, then mark the results with the included scripts.
- Use the inbox and prompts as a template for your own email routine. Leave out personal or confidential details before you paste real emails into any AI tool.
- Please don't present these files as real business records.

## Limits
- One run per assistant, on 9 October 2026, using the plans we had: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Max and the free Gemini plan. The results are not a general ranking of AI models.
- We did not measure how long the same work takes by hand, so we make no time-saving claims.
- AI tools change often. Your results may differ.
